# Round 6: four designs, ranked blind

The white restyle was built first (baseline D, commit e9b76a0), then three
designer subagents restyled the same product from the same brief with one
direction each, in their own worktrees, forbidden to touch the rig, the rubric,
the checks or the fixtures.

- A: dense instrument.
- B: cinema, media first.
- C: editorial monochrome.
- D: baseline (the first white pass).

Every candidate was shot with the same rig at 1280 and compared four ways in one
blind call per screen. Letters are shuffled per panel; the maps live in
`judges/inbox/round-6/map-<tag>.json` and were kept out of the panel directory
so a letter cannot be resolved while the call is being written.

## Verdicts

| screen | first | second | third | fourth |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| empty-upload | C editorial | D baseline | B cinema | A dense |
| results-6-clips | B cinema | C editorial | D baseline | A dense |
| review-theater | A dense | C editorial | B cinema | D baseline |

Mean rank: C 1.67, B 2.33, A 3.0, D 3.0.

## What the judges valued

Editorial won the upload screen on one idea: a black action, a settings list
built on a label gutter with no card chrome, and a page that reads like a page.
It placed second twice on the strength of its type scale (a 28px panel title,
right-aligned tabular numbers under a pulled-out score).

Cinema won the results screen with the cleanest action hierarchy: one black
Download, plain-text header utilities, one outlined Review clips, and the
filename sitting inline with the clip count.

Dense won the theater with an enclosure: a hairline under the decision row and a
legend that names every chord the buttons advertise.

## What disqualified the leaders as shipped

Editorial, despite the best mean rank, had removed Auto-cut, Zoom cuts and Edit
text from the inspector. A design that deletes product features cannot land, so
it went back to its designer with the judges' notes, the restored control list,
and the real fixture: full spoken-sentence headlines of 38 to 90 characters,
real scores of 29.7 to 37.2, real clip ranges.

Dense broke at 1280 on the upload screen: the drop zone clipped off the right
edge. Cinema lost the upload screen to two focal points and a near-invisible
drop boundary, and its theater placed third on control heights of 27px against
44px everywhere else.

## Method notes

- A call carries four images, one per design, plus the rubric. No candidate is
  labelled as the original and none is a reference.
- The rubric is the same eight categories the absolute pass uses, so a ranking
  and a score talk about the same things.
- Ranking is per screen on purpose. A single call over four whole products
  produces a taste verdict, not evidence.
